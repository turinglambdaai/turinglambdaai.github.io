# About jrtx.site

**English** · [简体中文](ABOUT.zh-CN.md)

jrtx.site introduces independent products, writing and reusable libraries. It does not provide a universal registration, activation or checkout service. Each product’s user documentation owns its actual availability and data handling. Most applications are still development previews.

Browse by task, then read installation and known limits on the product page. Library users can start with English or Chinese onboarding in Rivet, Glaze, Keel, Permit and Commerce. Chinese books and third-party archives retain their original content language; a complete English edition is not implied.

The page uses browser storage to remember language/theme and loads a Baidu analytics script; it must not be described as having no third-party analytics. Product links, GitHub, WeChat and external content have their own data policies. Clearing browser site data resets local preferences.

Internal target pricing and investment plans are not customer offers here. A product card does not make an unopened hosted or purchase flow available. Report product problems in that product’s Issues. Use [site Issues](https://github.com/turlamai/turlamai.github.io/issues) for broken links, copy or accessibility problems, after removing private information.
